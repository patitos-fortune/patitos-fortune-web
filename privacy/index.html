The short version

This is a simple website. It has no accounts, forms or advertising, and we do not use advertising or social-media trackers. We measure visits in aggregate and do not try to identify or profile individual visitors. This site does not set cookies of its own; see Cookies below.

What happens when you visit

Hosting. The files of this site are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. and GitHub B.V.). GitHub's documentation says that when someone visits a GitHub Pages site, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account. GitHub has not published how long these logs are kept. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Delivery. The site is delivered through Cloudflare, so your requests pass through Cloudflare's network. Cloudflare handles information such as your IP address, the page you request and technical details of your connection to deliver the site and keep it secure, and may keep logs. Cloudflare describes how long it keeps this information only in general terms, and says that for visitors to a customer's site it processes personal data on the customer's behalf. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

We handle this information because it is necessary to deliver the site and keep it secure. This is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR). [[PUBLICATION BLOCKER: legal basis provisional, to be confirmed by the owner or counsel]]

Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how the site is used, in aggregate: which pages are visited, visitors' countries, the pages they came from, their browsers and devices, and how quickly pages load. We use these statistics only to understand and improve the site, not for advertising, and we do not combine them with other information about you. This is also our legitimate interest. [[PUBLICATION BLOCKER: legal basis provisional, to be confirmed by the owner or counsel]]

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics does not use cookies or browser storage to track users or collect usage metrics, does not "fingerprint" individuals for analytics, and does not track individual users across its customers' sites. Cloudflare's documentation also states that it does not collect or use visitors' personal data. Your IP address is still handled when you connect to our site through Cloudflare, as described above. Cloudflare says detailed measurements are kept for about seven days and then reduced to a sample, and that statistics can be viewed for the previous six months.

Cookies

Analytics. This site does not set cookies of its own, and Cloudflare states that its Web Analytics works without cookies or browser storage for tracking users or collecting usage metrics.

Security. Separately, Cloudflare says its security features, such as bot protection, can set cookies on sites it delivers. When we last checked, no cookies were set on this site's pages. If that changes, we will update this page.

Transfers outside the EU

Cloudflare and GitHub may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Each states that it relies on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and on the EU standard contractual clauses for such transfers.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union you have rights over your personal data, including access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection. You can object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests. To use a right with us, write to hello@patitosfortune.com. Because the statistics we see are aggregate, we may not be able to identify you in them. You can also ask Cloudflare and GitHub about the information they hold.
