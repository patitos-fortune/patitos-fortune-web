- **Authorized use:** optimized, cropped promotional artwork on patitosfortune.com (the Games & Apps pages), shown with text stating that the game is in development and no preview is available yet.
- **Duration:** until withdrawn. On withdrawal the files are removed from the current site; copies in repository history or already downloaded may remain.
- **Rights:** all rights held by the relevant rights holder remain reserved. No general third-party licence is granted. This authorization is limited to the use above and does not change the Rummy project's licence or allow wider distribution of its source assets.
- **Derivative:** the images here are promotional derivatives only. The original artwork files are not stored in this repository.
